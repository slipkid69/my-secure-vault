Jordan Peterson – The Weeping Harlequin: A Jungian Wizard of Pretentious Skubalon. In the theatre of trembling wisdom, Where mirrors reflect only themselves, There dances the Weeping Harlequin, In a suit of red and blue spells. His lapels stitched from Jungian riddles, His cuffs woven tight with archetypes, He juggles twelve flaming rulebooks, While sobbing in shadowed spotlights. "Clean your chaos!" he shrieks at the wind, While his shoes squeak with Freudian guilt. He builds ladders made of trauma, To climb the fortress his psyche built. He speaks in encrypted syllables, Where syntax sobs and logic weeps, "The dragon of ideology is dreaming In your room where your shadow sleeps." Masonic murmurs lace his cuffs— Blue lodge buttons, all aglow. He lectures through a teardrop lens, Where esoteric teacups overflow. With one hand he paints a lion, With the other he mothers a snake, "The child is the father of order!" he cries, while his ego starts to quake. A pageboy of chaos, a squire of smoke, He psychoanalyses air, Pulling tissues from his archetype hat, While whispering, "Life isn’t fair." He weeps for logos, but preaches self, He names the myth but forgets the name, He sets his soul on Jungian shelves, And baptises shadows in blame. His gospel is weeping geometry, His prayer a serpentine wail, He sees himself in every myth, Yet vanishes when others fail. "The lobster is an icon of will!" He sobs while steaming in his pot, "Order is down your trousers, But only if truth is not." The crowd claps, confused and inspired, "So deep! So true! So... Jung!" But no one dares ask what he meant, Or why he licks his rhetorical tongue. He draws maps of symbolic flatulence, He sculpts suffering into slides, He warns: "If you don’t clench your cheeks, The archetype leaks out your sides." He calls for fire, yet worships fog, He beckons dragons, fears their flame, And every tear he sheds in public Adds a badge to his crying name. He weeps not for truth but image, A tear-shaped flag unfurled, For in the suit of the Harlequin, He hides from the real world. So beware the guru of waterworks, The seer of symbolic snot, For though he claims to tame your chaos, He sells the tears he's got. Behind the sobbing archetype, Beyond the serpent's grin, There waits no saviour of the psyche— Just a wet, weeping Harlequin. Behind the velvet curtain of the mind, A jester twitches, tightly lined. Each tear not drawn from psychic depth, But zapped up from the perineum’s breadth. He speaks of dragons and archetype blame, While volts arc softly across his shame. His sobs, though deep, are factory-timed— Each sniffle… corporate, pantomimed. So next you see him start to shake, It may not be a soul that breaks— But just a bzzzzt from backstage tech, Administered to keep him wrecked.

Order remains elusive. Flatulence remains symbolic.
