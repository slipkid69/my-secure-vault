The Room Where the Terralotl Waits

There is a room not far from here,
Though grown-ups say it's make-believe.
But if you close your eyes just right,
The Terralotl starts to breathe.

It isn't made of iron or stone—
The room is built from giggles, light,
A blanket fort, a bedtime hum,
A whisper floating through the night.

Inside, the Terralotl hums,
Its cheeks a glow of gentle pink,
Its tail curls round a dreaming cloud,
Its paws dip softly in the sink.

(It likes to wash its bubble cups—
Yes, bubble cups! From honey tea.
And if you’re kind and very still,
It might just make a cup for thee.)

The books it reads are upside-down,
It giggles when it gets to “zoo.”
It paints the walls with stardust dots,
And sometimes hums a tune for you.

You may not see it with your eyes,
But every time you laugh or care—
Each time you hug your teddy bear—
The Terralotl knows you're there.

And late at night, when grown-ups sleep,
It tiptoes through the wishing seam,
To curl beside your sleepy breath
And snuggle softly in your dream.

So if you feel alone someday,
Or school feels big, or skies are grey—
Just call its name inside your heart.
The Terralotl’s never far away.

Nimori of Hollowdrop Bay

Nimori is a child of the Terralotl kin, born near the crystal tides of Hollowdrop Bay, where laughter echoes through coral tunnels and the stars glisten like lanterns under the sea-glass sky.

Nimori is known for their bright gaze, musical chirps, and a heart that glows softly when stories are told. Some say Nimori was the first to hear the Resonance Bell that called the Terralotl out of hiding. Others believe Nimori carries inside them a forgotten melody — the kind that once stirred the ocean to dance.

Lullaby of the Tides

Sleep, little Nimori, safe in the tide,
Where moonlight and kelp gently collide.
Cradle of stars and a whispering shell,
You carry the songs the elders still tell.

Breathe in the shimmer, exhale the sea foam,
For even the waves know you’re never alone.
Dream of the coral and drift with the stream,
Nimori, beloved, child of the gleam.
